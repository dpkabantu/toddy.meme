Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

toddy.meme is an information website. It has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no advertising and no analytics scripts of its own, and it does not set cookies. Only the free TODDY games save your progress in your own browser (see section 5).

1. What this website collects

This website does not ask you for, and does not collect, personal information. You can read every page without signing up, logging in or connecting a wallet.

2. Technical data handled by our hosting provider

Like every website, toddy.meme is delivered through a hosting and security provider (Cloudflare). To deliver pages and protect the site from attacks and abuse, that provider processes standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and timestamps. It may keep standard server logs and aggregated traffic statistics. Its handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy.

3. Fonts loaded from Google

The site uses typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) to download them, so Google receives your IP address and standard request information, under Google’s privacy policy.

4. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set cookies. The information pages do not use browser storage; the games do, only on your device, as explained in section 5. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary security cookies in some situations, for example to tell real visitors from automated traffic.

5. TODDY games (Moon Brew and The Night Shift)

The games at toddy.meme/games are free and run entirely in your browser. You do not need an account, an email address or a wallet to play, and there are no tokens, rewards or prizes.

  • Saved on your device only: your progress, scores, streaks and settings (for example sound and text size) are stored in your browser’s local storage, so you can continue later. They are not sent to us. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes them.
  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking: the games set no cookies, show no advertising and send no play statistics. If we ever add anonymous play statistics, we will update this section first and offer a way to switch them off.
  • Sharing is your choice: when you tap Share, your device’s own share menu or the platform you pick (for example X or Telegram) receives the text or picture you choose to share. Copy link and Save image stay on your device.
  • Bug reports: “Report a bug” opens our public Telegram group. Anything you post there is visible to its members and handled under Telegram’s privacy policy.
  • Stay safe: TODDY games never ask for your wallet, seed phrase or any payment.

The games are in beta. If we later add optional player accounts (a TODDY Passport) or online leaderboards, we will update this page and explain what is collected and how to delete it before that starts.

6. Wallets, seed phrases and private keys

This website never asks you to connect a wallet, sign a message, approve a transaction or enter a seed phrase or private key. Anyone who asks you to do so in the name of TODDY is not TODDY. Use only the links on the official links page.

7. Links to other websites and social platforms

This site links to social platforms and other third-party websites (for example X, Telegram, Instagram, TikTok, Medium, Reddit and Discord). When you follow those links, the third party’s own privacy policy applies, and we do not control what they collect.

8. Children

This website is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. If this changes

If we add features that collect personal data (for example player accounts or online leaderboards), we will update this page and explain what is collected, why, and how to remove it, before that collection starts.

10. Your rights and contact

Because this website does not collect personal data itself, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Requests about data held by Cloudflare or Google must be made to them directly. For any other privacy question, contact us through the official channels listed on the official links page.
