How Season 1 works

    Season points are a record of your activity. They have no cash value. The numbers are a draft and can change before the Season opens.

    Tonight’s challenges

    Only tonight’s challenge counts for Season points. Practice, Story and Free play inside the games are just for fun.

    Weekly missions

    A weekly mission stays open all week, so you can do it any day. Finish it in its week for full points. Finish it later and you still get half.
