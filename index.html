How To Buy $TODDY

01

Get a Solana Wallet

Download Phantom Wallet from phantom.app — it is free and takes 2 minutes to set up on your phone or browser.

02

Buy Solana (SOL)

Buy SOL from any exchange like Binance, Coinbase or Kraken. Send it to your Phantom wallet address.

03

Go to Jupiter or Raydium

Visit jup.ag or raydium.io — Connect your Phantom wallet by clicking "Connect Wallet".

04

Swap SOL for $TODDY

Paste the $TODDY contract address, enter the amount of SOL you want to swap and click Swap. Done! You now hold $TODDY ☕🌙
