About Moon Brew

The game

Moon Brew is a cozy puzzle in Toddy’s night café. Free to play, no account, no wallet, no ads.

Credits

Game design, code and story: the TODDY team with Claude (Anthropic). Art: made with Nano Banana and approved by the TODDY team. Sound effects are made in code. Toddy, Milo and the night visitors are original TODDY characters.

Privacy

Your progress (stars, scores, streak, settings) is saved only on this device. If “Share anonymous play stats” is on in Settings, the game may send simple counts such as which tray was finished and how long it took, with a random number instead of your name. No personal details, no advertising trackers. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Stay safe

TODDY games never ask for your wallet, seed phrase, private key or payment. Anyone who does is not TODDY. This game is a free beta: things may change and progress could be reset.

How to brew

Tap a bean

It rolls where its tip points. Clear every bean.

Bumps

Blocked beans bump. Three bumps burns the brew.

Order ticket

Grind the glowing colour first for the third star.

Sleepy bean

Tap once to wake it, then it can roll.

Ice cube

Melts as you grind other beans.

Sugar pair

Two beans roll together. Both paths must be clear.

Milk swirl

Turns a rolling bean to its right.
